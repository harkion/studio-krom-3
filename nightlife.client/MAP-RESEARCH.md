# Eindhoven story research

Collected 8 October 2026 for the second portfolio review and the client discussion on 9 October. These are public first-person accounts used as secondary research. They are NOT interviews conducted by Fahri or the group. Neither has been independently verified. Keep them as short third-person source summaries, not invented interview answers or recorded testimony.

## 1. Early-morning harassment on Veldmaarschalk Montgomerylaan

Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/eindhoven/comments/196ro8p/
Title: Unpleasant experience. Posted 14 January 2024.

The author describes walking along Veldmaarschalk Montgomerylaan around 07:20, when it was dark. A stranger asked for directions and exposed himself. The author walked away and describes fear and uncertainty about how to respond.

Map scope: the street is explicitly named. The exact spot is unknown. Use a street overview marker, not a bench, address or supposed incident pin. This was an early-morning journey to university, not a confirmed night out. It fits broader safety after dark, with that limitation stated.

Research questions raised: How can a story communicate uncertainty without blaming the person? What information would someone want after an unwanted encounter? These are questions for future participants, not answers from this author.

## 2. Repeated harassment in Eindhoven and the Boschdijk area

Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/Netherlands/comments/1nfykio/
Title: Harassed almost every time I walk alone, is this normal? Posted 13 September 2025.

The author describes repeated unwanted attention and changes to their clothing. In a follow-up, they mention Woensel, Strijp in the evening and the Boschdijk area.

Map scope: Boschdijk is one broad area mentioned in an account covering multiple places. A street-centre marker is only an overview anchor. Do not say one particular incident happened at that marker. The account is about everyday harassment, including evening experiences, rather than specifically leaving a nightclub.

Research questions raised: How does repeated harassment change someone's routines? How should a map communicate experiences that span several streets? These remain questions to ask, not findings from an interview.

## Before public story installation use

Agree the reuse approach with the teacher/client. Ask the author for consent before adapting their account as a personal installation story, audio or first-person narrative. A public post is not consent to being interviewed or featured in this project. Do not include usernames, personal routes, homes or guessed identities. Ask participants what location precision they are comfortable with. People should be able to skip questions or stop an interview.

For real interviews, possible prompts:

- Would you feel comfortable describing an experience relevant to safety after dark? You can leave out any details.
- What about the surroundings affected how you felt?
- Did it change how you use that place afterwards?
- What would you want someone viewing the installation to understand?
- Would you want any part of this shared, and at what location detail?

No outreach has been sent. Do not invent responses or present these prompts as an interview transcript.
